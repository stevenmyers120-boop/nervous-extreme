Rules and controls

Classic: the conductor wins if Nervous is called before level 5 finishes; the challenger wins by completing all five. Surprise Truth/Dare: the challenger wins by answering or completing; the conductor wins if Nervous is called. If the conductor calls NERVOUS, the challenger earns two points. The combined score unlocks new intensity stages at 3 and 7. Round 2 starts at Getting Personal; Round 3 starts at Extreme. Optional timers are atmospheric only and never affect points. From Getting Personal onward, physical challenges are deliberately included in the rotation. First player to the selected winning score wins. Either player can end and reset without a penalty.

One-phone mode is offline. In two-phone mode, game state is shared directly between paired phones. Surprise questions and dares are spoken, not recorded. Do not record or share private content without permission. Never treat silence as consent.
